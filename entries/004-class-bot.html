Entry 004: The bot that attended my classes

When
first year of undergrad, COVID, online classes on Blackboard
Problem
I got fed up with joining every class myself.
Approach
I mapped my timetable and wrote a Python script with Selenium that opened each class automatically, on schedule.
What broke
the bot found buttons by their element names on the front end. Blackboard changed something on their side, and the whole thing collapsed.
Fixed it? No. The pandemic ended first.

Fixed it? No. Online classes ended about six months later, so the pandemic beat me to it.

  • Lesson: if you build on something you don't control, it will change without asking you.
